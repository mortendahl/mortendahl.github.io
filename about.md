I am currently VP of Blockchain Technology at [Zama](https://www.zama.org), where I lead protocol architecture for the [Zama Protocol](https://docs.zama.org/protocol/zama-protocol-litepaper) and the underlying [fhEVM](https://github.com/zama-ai/fhevm). The protocol brings confidential smart contracts to existing public blockchains using fully homomorphic encryption and threshold MPC. It has been live on Ethereum mainnet since the end of 2025 and now powers Zama's [financial products](https://app.zama.org/), including confidential tokens, vaults, and swapping.

My background is a mix of software engineering, cryptography, privacy, and machine learning. A common thread has been turning advanced techniques like secure multiparty computation and homomorphic encryption into tools developers can actually use, first for machine learning and now for blockchains.

Before Zama I co-founded Cape Privacy, which later became [Cape.ai](https://cape.ai), and was its chief scientist. I led the cryptography team at [Snips](https://snips.ai/) working on privacy-preserving machine learning, and worked in data science at [Datadog](https://www.datadoghq.com/) and in software engineering at [Zenly](https://www.zenly.com/). Along the way I created [TF Encrypted](https://github.com/tf-encrypted/tf-encrypted) for encrypted deep learning in TensorFlow, which has been used, among others, for privacy-preserving genomics research.

I hold an MSc in theoretical computer science and a PhD in cryptography from Aarhus University.
